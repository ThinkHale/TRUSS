# Content and IP rights

What TRUSS owns, what it summarizes, and what has to be true before someone buys it. An acquirer's
diligence team will ask for each item below; this is the inventory to hand them, and the list of
what is not yet done.

## What the product is made of

| Asset | Where | Rights position |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Application code | `src/`, `scripts/`, `supabase/migrations/`, `tests/` | Written for TRUSS. Ownership sits with whoever wrote it until assigned to the company — see *Still to do*. |
| TRUSS Method and doctrine | `TRUSS_Sales_Intelligence_Repository/docs/` | Original writing. Cites sources; does not reproduce them. |
| Knowledge units, scenarios, objections, campaign patterns, eval cases | `TRUSS_Sales_Intelligence_Repository/data/*.jsonl` | Original structured records, each tied to source ids in the registry. |
| Compiled knowledge base | `TRUSS_AI_Knowledge_Base_v*.md`, `src/lib/truss/knowledge/corpus.generated.ts` | Generated from the two rows above. |
| Eight-week program | `docs/06_training/eight_week_curriculum.md`, `src/lib/truss/curriculum.ts` | Original. The Spanish in `curriculum.ts` is an original translation. |
| Brand | `TRUSS_brand_assets_final/`, "TRUSS" name and lockup | See *Still to do* — no trademark filing is recorded in the repository. |
| Customer material | `knowledge_documents` per tenant | Belongs to the customer. Held on their instructions; exported and deleted with their account. Never shared across tenants (RLS; `tests/db`). |

## The source registry

`TRUSS_Sales_Intelligence_Repository/data/source_registry.csv` records every source the knowledge base
draws on, with a rights status under the policy in `docs/00_governance/evidence_rights_and_maintenance.md`:

| Rights status | Sources | What TRUSS holds |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| `public` | 31 | Government, regulator, and standards material; public datasets |
| `public_summary` | 20 | Original summaries of public pages, in TRUSS's own words |
| `metadata_only` | 19 | Title, creator, URL, date, and original TRUSS notes — no content |
| `licensed` | 0 | — |
| `unknown` | 0 | — |

The 19 `metadata_only` sources are the books, podcasts, and channels (for example Huthwaite's SPIN
research, *Never Split the Difference*, *Fanatical Prospecting*, and several trade podcasts). The
policy is that TRUSS holds only its own notes about them and never their text, transcripts, or course
material. That policy is what keeps them `metadata_only`; it is worth a one-time audit that no knowledge
unit quotes one of them at length.

By evidence grade: 35 A (statute, regulator, standards, peer-reviewed), 19 B (transparent surveys and
platform data), 16 C (books, podcasts, practitioners). No grade D source is ingested.

## Names that are someone else's

- **SPIN** is a Huthwaite International trademark. TRUSS teaches discovery in its own terms (situation,
  problem, implication, outcome, decision, constraint) and should not use "SPIN" in product copy or
  marketing.
- **Framework names from practitioner books** — the program document's Week 5 refers to "ACORN
  objection diagnosis." The in-product curriculum describes the behavior without the name. Before any
  marketing uses a named framework, confirm who owns it.
- **CRM names** (JobNimbus, AccuLynx, ServiceTitan, Leap, HubSpot, Salesforce) appear only to say TRUSS
  can receive their data. That is nominative use; do not imply partnership or endorsement without an
  agreement.

## Model output

Coach answers, scorecards, campaign drafts, and research briefs are generated by OpenAI models. Under
OpenAI's business terms the customer owns output, and customer API data is not used for training by
default. Confirm both against the terms in force when the company is sold, and keep the account under
the company's name.

## Still to do before an exit

1. **Assign the IP to the company.** Every founder, employee, and contractor who wrote code, doctrine,
   or brand assets signs an invention and IP assignment to the operating entity. AI-assisted work is
   covered the same way: the person directing it assigns it.
2. **File the trademark.** Search and file "TRUSS" (and the lockup) for software and training services
   in the classes that cover them. A sales-training company named TRUSS already existing would be a
   material problem to find now rather than in diligence.
3. **Audit the `metadata_only` sources** against the knowledge units that cite them, and record the
   audit in the repository's changelog.
4. **Put the rights policy in the customer agreement.** Customer knowledge documents are the customer's;
   TRUSS may process them only to serve that customer; they are deleted on termination. The code already
   behaves this way — the contract should say so.
5. **Keep the registry current.** New sources enter with a rights status; `unknown` blocks ingestion, as
   the policy already states.
